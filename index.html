How do I reset my portal password?
Go to Profile and click "Change Password". A reset link will be sent to your registered email address. If you no longer have access to that email, visit the ICT unit with a valid ID.
How do I pay my fees online?
Open the Payments section and click "Make Payment". You can pay the full fee or choose an instalment plan. Always download your receipt after a successful payment.
What is the pass mark for CA and exams?
Continuous Assessment contributes 30% and the exam 70% of your final grade. The minimum pass mark for each course is 40/100 (grade D and above).
When can I download my certificate?
Certificates are issued once you complete all courses in your programme with a cumulative GPA of at least 3.00. You will receive an email when yours is ready.
